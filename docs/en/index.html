Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I download a Calaméo publication as a PDF?

Install the extension, open the publication in the Calaméo reader, and click its Download button. The complete PDF is saved to your device.

Can I save only selected pages?

Yes. Click Print or press Ctrl+P, choose “Save as PDF” in the browser dialog, then enter the page numbers you want.

Is the PDF searchable?

Pages are assembled as images. OCR text, links, and interactive media are not added.
